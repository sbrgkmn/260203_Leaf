# Published comparison images

Unaltered figure crops from the user-supplied *Metamorphic Leaves*, Sabri Gokmen, Leonardo 53(5), 2020, pp. 522-528. Copyright Sabri Gokmen as credited in the supplied figures.

`*-final.png` comes from Figure 7. Buttercup and white oak development come from Figures 4 and 5; the other `*-series.png` crops come from the corresponding Figure 6 rows. Figure 6 skips some intermediate operations. Its printed numbers must not be interpreted as column-aligned with the app's generated steps.

These local reference images support comparison with the author's supplied paper. The app's three-stage framework and numeric recipes are reconstructions and extensions, not extracted original parameters.
