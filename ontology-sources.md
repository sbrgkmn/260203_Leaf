Both supplied PDFs (IPWN_Bockemuhl.pdf and MOP_Bockemuhl.pdf) contain the same photographed spread at PDF page 7, printed pages 22–23: “Morphic Movements in Individual Leaf Development,” Figure 1 (Cardamine hirsuta).

- Shooting: a pointed structure emerges and grows directionally. Implemented as a single narrow E form, lengthening without recursive lateral shoots.
- Segmenting: the source calls this “dividing”; the pointed growth activity multiplies. Implemented by enabling successive E/C shoot pairs.
- Spreading: the blade fills between protrusions; points become rounder. Implemented by reducing inward C displacement and increasing rounding while retaining three shoot pairs.
- Stemming: the source calls this “elongation”; the leaf base lengthens and narrows into a stalk. Implemented as explicit basal extension after the E/C blade forms, preserving the blade shape.

These support distinguishing individual development from a series of different mature forms; they do not establish this exact circular plot as a measured sequence.
